What is the meaning of the Dagaz Rune?

Dagaz (ᛞ) is the rune of daylight, transformation, and awakening. It symbolizes the moment when darkness gives way to light, bringing clarity, hope, and new opportunities. Often associated with breakthroughs and positive change, Dagaz encourages personal growth, balance, and a fresh perspective. It reminds us that every ending can become a new beginning.
